ABOUT US · CORPORATE OVERVIEW

Building Trusted Medical & Aesthetic Supply Connections in Mongolia

Khundet Nobilis LLC is a Mongolia-based company focused on sourcing, introducing, and developing B2B distribution opportunities for international medical consumables and professional aesthetic products.

OUR MISSION

Responsible Sourcing & Practical Market Development

To connect reputable South Korean and international manufacturers with Mongolian hospitals, aesthetic clinics, and wholesale buyers through transparent communication and step-by-step channel development.

OUR VISION

Becoming a Trusted Multi-Brand B2B Distributor in Mongolia

Building upon our initial pilot sales foundation to secure official distributorship agreements and establish a reliable multi-brand supply portfolio across Ulaanbaatar.
