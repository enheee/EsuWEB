01 / COMPANY INTRODUCTION · 기업 소개 · КОМПАНИЙН ТАНИЛЦУУЛГА

Connecting Global Medical Supply & Aesthetic Solutions with Mongolia

ENGLISH

Khundet Nobilis LLC is a Mongolia-based multi-brand B2B sourcing and distribution development company focused on clinical medical consumables, professional aesthetic threads, hyaluronic acid fillers, and derma-cosmetics. Our initial pilot sales of selected Korean products provided practical insight into customer and clinic demand in Ulaanbaatar, forming the foundation for long-term official manufacturer partnerships.

한국어 (KOREAN)

Khundet Nobilis LLC(훈데트 노빌리스)는 몽골 울란바토르를 거점으로 의료용 소모품, 프로페셔널 에스테틱(PDO 리프팅 실, 필러) 및 더마 코스메틱을 발굴·도입하고 병원·클리닉 B2B 유통망을 개발하는 멀티 브랜드 기업입니다. 한국 제품의 초기 파일럿 판매(Pilot Sales)를 통해 현지 수요를 확인하였으며, 해외 제조사와의 신뢰 기반 공식 유통 파트너십을 구축하고 있습니다.

Core Medical & Professional Aesthetic Product Lines

1. Medical Consumables (의료용 소모품 · Эмнэлгийн хэрэгсэл)

Clinical surgical tapes and hospital consumables, including candidate reference 3M™ Micropore™ Surgical Tape (Tan, REF 1533-1) (under portfolio evaluation).

2. Professional Aesthetic Threads (프로페셔널 PDO 실 · Мэргэжлийн PDO утас)

Absorbable Polydioxanone (PDO) thread lines for licensed aesthetic clinics, including candidate reference TIME MACHINE PDO Thread (타임머신) (under portfolio evaluation).

3. Professional Medical Aesthetics (메디컬 에스테틱 필러 · Гоо заслын филлэр)

Professional hyaluronic acid fillers for aesthetic centers, including candidate reference SEDY FILL 60cc (24 mg/cc HA Body Filler) (under portfolio evaluation).

4. Derma-Cosmetics & Post-Procedure Care (더마 코스메틱 · Арьс арчилгаа)

Barrier-repair skincare and post-procedure recovery products tailored for Mongolian clinics, salons, and wholesale channels (open for new brand onboarding).
